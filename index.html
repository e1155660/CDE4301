1. Objective and Context

1.1 Objective

The main objective of our project is to automate a critical task in the medication loading process of the Drug Dispensing System 2 (DDS-2, Video 1) at National University Hospital (NUH) Pharmacy. Specifically, our project aims to reduce the time required for medication restocking and minimise human errors by developing a solution capable of automatically counting medication blisters and recognising and verifying medication information, such as batch numbers and expiry dates. By reducing reliance on repetitive manual counting and data entry, the proposed solution aims to improve the efficiency, accuracy and reliability of the medication loading process while maintaining patient safety.
